# Phase 4 — Public catalogue and frontend foundation

The overall delivery plan remains unchanged: Phase 4 delivers the public catalogue.

## 4A — Account and workspace frontend

- Sign up, sign in, email verification, password recovery, and safe user-facing states.
- Mandatory administrator TOTP enrollment, verification, and recovery-code sign-in.
- Protected student/admin workspace foundations, profile, security, and role-aware navigation.

## 4B — Public catalogue

- Responsive public landing page, published exam and package listings, and detail pages.
- Subjects and tests are database-driven; draft content is never exposed publicly.
- Free-test discovery is database-driven: a test qualifies only when the test, exam, and linked zero-price product are all published.
- Starting a test requires sign-in. Test-engine execution remains Phase 6.

## Out of scope

- Admin content authoring and review workflows remain Phase 5.
- Timer, autosave, attempt execution, and submission remain Phase 6.
- Results, commerce, protected materials, and complete operational dashboards remain in their original phases.
